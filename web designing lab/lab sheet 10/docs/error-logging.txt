Q16: ERROR PAGES AND LOGGING
- public/index.php catches exceptions, writes details with error_log(), and shows a
  generic 500 page. Unknown routes show the custom 404 page.
- config/config.php sets display_errors=0 and enables error logging.
- Ensure storage/logs is writable by the web-server user.
- In Apache VirtualHost, configure ErrorLog to a protected log file.
- Never show stack traces, SQL errors or credentials to visitors.
