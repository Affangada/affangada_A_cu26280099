# Project Specification — Green Spoon Cafe

## Fictional client
Green Spoon Cafe is a neighbourhood cafe serving seasonal vegetarian meals, artisan bakery items and responsibly sourced coffee.

## Goal
Create a welcoming, accessible website that introduces the cafe, presents its main offerings, builds trust through testimonials and lets visitors request a table.

## Target audience
Local residents, students, office workers and visitors aged approximately 18–45 who value fresh food, relaxed spaces and convenient online information.

## Main requirements
- Clear home page and navigation
- Menu cards for bowls, bakery items and drinks
- About/story section and team-oriented brand message
- Customer testimonial cards with visible star ratings
- Booking request form with browser-side validation
- Contact details and responsive Google Maps embed
- Social links, SEO metadata and responsive design

## Scope and limitations
The project is a front-end prototype. A production version would require a server/API, database, spam protection, real booking confirmation, privacy policy and client-owned social accounts.
